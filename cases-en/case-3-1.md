# Case 3.1 (China): Banking — Industrial Bank built an agent platform scaling from 200+ to 345 agents in a year

**Industry / scenario**: (Large enterprise: 8,245 tech staff at end-2025, 13.88% of headcount) Industrial Bank (兴业银行), a national joint-stock commercial bank. Scenarios include a bank-wide agent platform, AI digital customer service, an R&D assistant, and risk-control uses such as AML and anti-fraud.

**Pain point**: Banks are accelerating LLM adoption; risk screening, anti-fraud and AML are the main directions. Industrial Bank set up an "AI+" action leadership group and needed to consolidate scattered AI applications onto one platform.

**AI approach**: Published its "AI+ Action Plan (2025–2027)"; built an agent platform with 200+ agents listed by H1 2025; deployed omnichannel AI digital customer service and an R&D assistant across the bank's development lines; reinforced the "digital security line" in AML and anti-fraud.

**Outcome**: Per the bank's H1-2025 report (cited by Economic Information Daily), it intercepted ¥504M in fraud-related funds in H1 2025 and protected ¥803M in potential victim transactions (the source does not state AI's specific contribution to these figures). Per China News Service (Sept 2026), the bank was running 345 agents across risk, marketing, service and operations, with 21 mainstream LLMs deployed and 2.1M+ daily calls. (Provenance: company self-reported, from the semi-annual report and media coverage of it.)

**Firm profile**: Banks and large financial institutions with many business lines, heavy risk-control and service pressure, needing centralized governance of many AI applications.

*Sources: Industrial Bank 2025 Semi-Annual Report (board-approved 2025-08-28); Economic Information Daily "银行大模型应用'加速跑'" (2025-09-10); China News Service, "兴业银行半年报观察" (2026-09-15); Industrial Bank 2025 Annual Report Summary (2026-03-26, scale basis) (self-reported / media paraphrase)*
