# Case 3.1 (China): Banking — Industrial Bank builds an AI agent platform, expanding from over 200 to 345 agents within a year

**Industry / scenario**: (Large enterprise: 8,245 technology staff at end-2025, 13.88% of total employees) Industrial Bank (兴业银行), a national joint-stock commercial bank. Scenarios include a bank-wide AI agent platform, AI digital customer service, an R&D assistant, and risk-control areas such as anti-money laundering and anti-fraud.

**Pain point**: The banking industry is accelerating large-model deployment, with risk screening and early warning, anti-fraud and anti-money laundering as the main application areas. Industrial Bank set up an AI+ Action (“人工智能+”行动) leading group and working group, and needed to bring its scattered AI applications together on a unified platform.

**AI solution**: Issued its AI+ Action Plan (2025–2027) (《“人工智能+”行动方案（2025—2027年）》); built an AI agent platform with over 200 agents listed in H1 2025; deployed omnichannel AI digital customer service; and rolled out an R&D assistant across the bank's R&D function. At the same time, it reinforced its digital security defence line (数字安保防线) in areas such as anti-money laundering and anti-fraud.

**Results**: According to Economic Information Daily, citing Industrial Bank's 2025 interim report, in H1 2025 the bank intercepted RMB 504 million in fraud-related funds and protected RMB 803 million in transaction funds of potential victims (the original does not state AI's specific contribution to these figures). According to a China News Service report in September 2026, the bank has built and is running 345 AI agents across risk, marketing, customer service, operations and other areas, has brought 21 mainstream large models online, and averages more than 2.1 million calls per day. (Basis: company self-reported — from the bank's interim report and media coverage of the interim report)

**Who this fits**: Banks and large financial institutions with many business lines, a need to manage a large number of AI applications centrally, and heavy risk-control and customer-service pressure.
