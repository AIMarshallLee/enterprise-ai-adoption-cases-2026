# Case 11.5 (Japan): Real estate — a Tokyo commercial property firm cuts contract drafting time ~50% with generative AI

**Industry / scenario**: (Mid-size: vendor case page states 100–299 employees) Family Corporation, headquartered in Ginza, Tokyo with Osaka and Fukuoka branches; the group calls itself a comprehensive business-use real estate company (income property, land use, wealth management, overseas property, redevelopment). Scenario: transaction documents and internal paperwork.

**Pain point**: Per the vendor case page, meeting minutes, approval documents and contract confirmation consumed time, hurting efficiency and crowding out sales and client service; contract drafting depended on individuals with no standardization; internal AI literacy was low, raising the adoption barrier.

**AI approach**: Adopted generative AI for contract drafting and automatic meeting minutes, plus training to deepen staff understanding. The stated plan: train all staff to use generative AI and extend it to investor proposals and research.

**Outcome**: The case page reports contract drafting time down ~50% and lighter burden from auto-generated minutes; accuracy and business quality improved for contracts and client communications; training improved information sharing and standardization (later items without quantified data). (Provenance: vendor-reported — DigiRise customer case page; the summary says "work time down ~50%" while the body says "contract drafting down ~50%"; we follow the body.)

**Firm profile**: Small/mid-size property firms and brokerages with heavy contract and disclosure paperwork where document quality depends on individual experience.
