# Case 26.5 (Japan): Food Service — A small Fukuoka restaurant run by 2–4 people takes reservation calls with AI; more than half of its roughly 150 calls a month are now handled automatically

**Industry / scenario**: (Small company: the source says the restaurant is run by 2–4 people) Daidokoro Youha (台所ようは), a restaurant in Daimyo, Chuo-ku, Fukuoka City, converted from a 50-year-old house. The scenario is automated answering of reservation calls.

**Pain point**: Only a few people handle everything from prep to service, so whenever a call came in during prep they had to stop what they were doing and the work could not move forward. The restaurant plays no background music, so a ringing phone spoiled the dining atmosphere. The house rule was that whoever could answer should answer as quickly as possible, but during the near-full peak from 19:00 to 20:00 they sometimes could not answer at all. Picking up the phone in the middle of a rush also left no room to handle calls calmly, and the restaurant had even received complaints about poor phone manner.

**AI solution**: From March 2024 the restaurant began trialling IVRy's "AI電話予約サービス" (AI phone reservation service). The service is integrated with Recruit's reservation management app "レストランボード" (Restaurant Board); AI answers calls automatically, so reservations can be accepted 24 hours a day. Reservations taken by the AI are synced automatically into Restaurant Board, and the system accepts reservations only for time slots that have availability in Restaurant Board.

**Results**: Of about 150 calls a month, more than half are now handled automatically. Before adoption, the manager worried that switching to AI phone answering would reduce reservations; in practice reservations did not fall, and reservations can now also be accepted outside business hours. The source gives no figures on savings in staff hours or labor costs. (Basis: vendor-reported, IVRy customer case study, the manager's own account)

**Who this fits**: Small restaurants and izakaya where a few people keep the whole place running, nobody can spare a hand to answer the phone at peak times, and reservations come mainly by phone.
