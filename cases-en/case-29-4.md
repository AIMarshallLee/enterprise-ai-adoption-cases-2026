# Case 29.4 (United Kingdom): Telecom — a ~300-staff full-fiber ISP added AI scheduling forecasts and a chatbot, cutting overall customer contact rate 29%

**Industry / scenario**: (Mid-size: ISPreview reported in Apr 2022 that Fibrus directly employs nearly 300 people; total headcount undisclosed since; the contact center has 130 staff across service, sales, billing and tech support) Fibrus Networks builds full-fiber to homes across Northern Ireland and northern England with £500M+ invested. Scenario: contact-center channel deflection, demand forecasting and scheduling.

**Pain point**: Systems failed weekly and reporting was poor; during peaks — or storms and high winds that cluster service problems — the team struggled to cope.

**AI approach**: Genesys contact-center platform with AI-driven forecasting and scheduling, chatbot deflection, and redesigned digital-first customer care.

**Outcome**: Customer contact rate down 29%, freeing capacity that contributed to ~£630,000 in staffing savings; NPS up 27 points, Trustpilot rating up 7 points (the case-page summary separately states Trustpilot went from 1.7 in 2022 to 4.2 — a different framing). (Provenance: vendor-reported.)

**Firm profile**: Regional broadband, cable and small ISPs whose fault volumes spike with weather and whose contact teams run to ~100 staff.
