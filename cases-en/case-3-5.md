# Case 3.5 (United States): Financial services (credit union) — a 150-person credit union freezes headcount growth after launching an AI digital assistant

**Industry / scenario**: (Mid-size: vendor case page cites 150 full-time employees serving 50,000+ members) Harvard Federal Credit Union (HFCU), founded 1939. Scenario: member-facing AI digital assistant on web and mobile, plus an employee-facing knowledge assistant.

**Pain point**: Per the vendor case page, HFCU struggled to scale service efficiently and offered no 24/7 support. EVP & Chief Experience Officer Tom Montilli expected to add a support rep every year, and more after adding chat.

**AI approach**: In November 2022 HFCU launched Posh's Digital Assistant on web and mobile, then an employee Knowledge Assistant centralizing information; voice assistant for after-hours and call volume is planned. Montilli emphasizes redeploying staff skills rather than layoffs, and no barriers when members want a human.

**Outcome**: Per the Posh case study, call volume and wait times fell (no figures given) with an 81% containment rate; Montilli says the department has frozen new hiring because slack absorbs growth. Posh's case-listing metrics card shows: 86% chat containment, 1,800 chats per month, abandonment down 3.4%, 1 FTE saved (undated card, inconsistent with the 81% in the body — both figures given side by side). (Provenance: vendor-reported; the credit union's 2024 Annual Report does not mention the project.)

**Firm profile**: Credit unions, community banks and small financial institutions with 100–200 staff and tens of thousands of members; tight service staffing, wanting nights/weekends coverage without annual hiring.
