# Case 25.6 (Australia, FAILURE CASE): Travel — a small tour operator outsourced its marketing blog to AI, invented a non-existent hot spring, and tourists showed up

**Industry / scenario**: (Small enterprise: Euronews calls it "a smaller boutique-type operator"; the owner describes a husband-and-wife business with sales staff; headcount undisclosed) Tasmania Tours, operated by Australian Tours and Cruises (NSW). Scenario: destination-marketing content on the company blog.

**Pain point**: The owner said the company must compete with bigger firms and keep website content fresh, but "we don't have the manpower to write enough content," so the work was outsourced to AI.

**Result**: An AI-written article invented "Weldborough Pass hot springs" in Tasmania — a place that doesn't exist. Tourists traveled there expecting to bathe and found nothing. The story made national and international news, forcing public corrections. The handbook files it under the largest failure category: "no human fact-checking before publishing AI output."

*Sources: Euronews travel coverage by Craig Tansley and follow-ups; ABC Tasmania reporting (media-reported)*
