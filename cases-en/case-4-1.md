# Case 4.1 (Sweden / Global): Retail (Home Furnishings) — AI bot Billie handles 47% of call-centre queries as 8,500 customer-service staff are retrained as remote interior design advisers

**Industry / scenario**: (Large enterprise: Reuters reported that Ingka has trained 8,500 call-centre employees as interior design advisers since 2021; total headcount not disclosed in the pages consulted) Home furnishings retail; an AI bot in the call centre, plus customer-service staff redeployed to paid remote design services.

**Pain point**: The source does not describe a pain point separately. Reuters reported that IKEA wants to offer more home improvement services and hand routine customer queries to AI.

**AI solution**: IKEA retailer Ingka Group (Reuters says the IKEA website is owned by Ingka) uses an AI bot called "Billie" to handle customer queries to its call centres; since 2021 it has also trained call-centre employees as interior design advisers who provide remote design services by phone and video. In the UK, for example, customers pay GBP 25 for a 45–60-minute interior design video consultation and a list of recommended products.

**Results**: Over the past two years, Billie handled 47% of customer queries to call centres; 8,500 call-centre employees were trained as interior design advisers; the remote interior design channel generated EUR 1.3 billion in revenue in Ingka's fiscal year 2022, 3.3% of total revenue, with a target of raising that to 10% by 2028. Asked whether AI would lead to job cuts, Ingka's HR lead Ulrika Biesert replied: "That's not what we're seeing right now." (Basis: media report; data provided by the company to Reuters)

**Who this fits**: Large retail chains with big customer-service centres that want to redeploy staff freed up by AI into value-added services such as design consultation and shopping guidance.

*Sources: Reuters, "Ikea bets on remote interior design as AI changes sales strategy" (2023-06-14, republished by FashionNetwork) (media report; data provided by the company to the media)*
