# Case 2.3 (China, German-owned): Manufacturing — German PCB maker's Jiangyou plant cuts delays 43% with AI dynamic scheduling

**Industry / scenario**: (Large enterprise: STARTEAM GLOBAL 1,000+ employees globally, 800+ at the Sichuan Jiangyou plant) PCB manufacturing / AI production scheduling for high-mix, low-volume production.

**Pain point**: The source describes no specific pain point beyond PAILOT's target environment of high-mix, low-volume manufacturing.

**AI approach**: STARTEAM GLOBAL's Jiangyou PCB plant connected PAILOT's AI production-planning platform via API to its existing MES/ERP, rescheduling in real time against order changes, machine availability and disruptions; STARTEAM says PAILOT technology is integrated into drilling and milling equipment and the MES.

**Outcome**: Delays down 43%, capacity utilization up 10%, changeover time down 15%, energy consumption down 24%. The talk's title claimed "utilization up to 20%," inconsistent with the abstract's 10%; the source does not explain the gap. (Provenance: vendor-reported — joint PAILOT/STARTEAM productronica 2025 talk abstract.)

**Firm profile**: High-mix low-volume PCB/electronics plants with MES/ERP in place that want higher utilization and fewer delays without new equipment.
