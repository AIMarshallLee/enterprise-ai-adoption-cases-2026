# Case 3.4 (Hong Kong SAR): Financial services (brokerage) — licensed broker replaces night-shift support with an LLM customer service bot, complaints down 80%

**Industry / scenario**: (Size undisclosed: SFC-licensed corporation in Hong Kong, headcount not public; third-party compilations of SFC registers show 9 licensed individuals) Securities brokerage; 24/7 online service for overseas clients and an "AI investor-relations officer."

**Pain point**: Waton Securities International, a licensed broker with 33 years in the industry, serves overseas clients whose inquiries involve account and trading data; slow replies erode trust. Before deployment, overseas service required 2 daytime and 5 night agents.

**AI approach**: Built on Amazon Bedrock with Anthropic Claude 3: Claude Sonnet and Haiku classify intent, responses are generated from a proprietary knowledge base with open-source embedding models; human agents review and enrich LLM output. An "AI investor relations officer" launched in the same period. From first contact to go-live took under 3 months.

**Outcome**: Response time from minutes to under 3 seconds, 24/7 coverage; complaint rate down 80%; 3 human agents plus the AI now cover the previous overseas workload, cutting overall operating cost 36.6%. (Provenance: vendor-reported — article bylined by AWS and published via TMTPost.)

**Firm profile**: Small and mid-size brokerages, futures firms and cross-border financial services with multi-timezone clients, night-shift coverage needs, and account/transaction-heavy inquiries.
