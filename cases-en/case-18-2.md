# Case 18.2 (Japan): Pharma R&D — Chugai Pharmaceutical built an internal generative-AI platform used by 1,000–1,500 people daily

**Industry / scenario**: (Large enterprise: 7,778 consolidated employees as of 2024-12-31) Chugai Pharmaceutical. Scenario: generative AI across R&D, clinical development and regulatory documents.

**Pain point**: DX lead Takao Suzuki names the industry's two structural problems: rising cost of creating a new drug and declining R&D productivity — reportedly ~96.8 months from clinical trial to approval with only ~11.8% trial success (industry figures cited by the speaker).

**AI approach**: Built on AWS Bedrock, a platform allowing multiple cloud LLMs under unified governance, rolled out as "Chugai AI Assistant" with weekly feature releases, plus internal idea-collection for use cases.

**Outcome**: 1,000–1,500 people use it daily. The internal call for generative-AI ideas collected ~900 proposals within months of launch; ~29 in PoC (ongoing or complete), 14 in active development or completed. (Provenance: company self-reported via media coverage of a 2024-11-21 case presentation. No hours-saved figures disclosed; clinical and regulatory uses described as envisioned, not deployed.)

**Firm profile**: Pharma firms with thousands of staff and heavy R&D/clinical documentation loads that want multi-model use on one governed platform, starting from internal idea collection and PoC screening.
