# Case 28.5 (United States, FAILURE CASE): Agriculture — vertical farm Bowery Farming raised $700M+ on computer vision and ML, then ceased operations

**Industry / scenario**: (Size undisclosed: at least 187 staff laid off per WARN notices at shutdown; $700M+ raised) Indoor vertical farming (controlled-environment agriculture) for leafy greens. Per 2018 coverage, Bowery's hydroponic systems were unified by its in-house BoweryOS, using computer vision and robotic automation guided by ML algorithms to monitor crops and growth variables around the clock; investor General Catalyst then saw the software/AI focus as the key differentiator.

**Pain point / what happened**: Bowery sold premium indoor-grown produce, but the sources attribute the shutdown to business-level causes — unit economics, demand and capital costs — not to the AI itself. The handbook files it under "non-technical business failure": the AI stack was real, the business was not viable.

**Result**: Operations ceased with WARN-notice layoffs of at least 187 staff despite $700M+ raised across rounds. The lesson the handbook draws: AI cannot rescue a business model whose unit economics don't work — evaluate cost accounts alongside any AI capability.

*Sources: WARN notices and business press coverage of the shutdown (TechCrunch, AgFunderNews, etc., Nov 2024); 2018 coverage of BoweryOS funding rounds (media-reported)*
