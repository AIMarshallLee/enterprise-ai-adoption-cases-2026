# Case 4.1 (Sweden/global): Retail (home furnishing) — AI bot Billie handles 47% of call-center inquiries; 8,500 agents retrained as remote interior design consultants

**Industry / scenario**: (Large enterprise: Reuters reports Ingka has retrained 8,500 call-center employees as interior design consultants since 2021; total headcount not given in the pages reviewed) Home-furnishing retail; call-center AI bot plus retraining agents into paid remote design services.

**Pain point**: The source describes no standalone pain point. Per Reuters, IKEA wanted to expand home-improvement services and hand routine inquiries to AI.

**AI approach**: IKEA's retail arm Ingka Group deployed an AI bot named "Billie" for call-center inquiries, while retraining call-center staff as remote interior design consultants since 2021. In the UK, for example, customers pay £25 for a 45–60 minute video design consultation plus a product recommendation list.

**Outcome**: Over two years Billie handled 47% of call-center inquiries; 8,500 call-center employees were retrained as design consultants; remote design generated €1.3B in FY2022 (3.3% of revenue), targeting 10% by 2028. Asked whether AI causes layoffs, Ingka's HR head Ulrika Biesert said "not that we see at the moment." (Provenance: media coverage — data provided by the company to Reuters.)

**Firm profile**: Large multi-location retailers with big call centers that want to redeploy staff freed by AI into value-added services such as design consulting.

*Source: Reuters "Ikea bets on remote interior design as AI changes sales strategy" (2023-06-14, via FashionNetwork) (media coverage of company-provided figures)*
