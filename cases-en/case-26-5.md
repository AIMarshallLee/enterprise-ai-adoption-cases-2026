# Case 26.5 (Japan): Restaurants — a 2–4 person Fukuoka restaurant automates reservations by phone; over half of ~150 monthly calls handled without staff

**Industry / scenario**: (Small enterprise: the source says the restaurant is run by 2–4 people) Daidokoro Yoha (台所ようは), a restaurant in Daimyo, Fukuoka City, converted from a 50-year-old house. Scenario: automated answering of reservation calls.

**Pain point**: With only a few people handling both prep and service, every call interrupted the work in progress. The dining room has no background music, so a ringing phone breaks the atmosphere. House rule: "whoever can, picks up fast" — but at the 19:00–20:00 near-full peak, sometimes nobody could. Even when answered mid-rush, they couldn't give proper attention.

**AI approach**: IVRy's AI phone-reservation service answers calls, handles booking requests and syncs the calendar.

**Outcome**: Over half of ~150 monthly calls are now handled automatically. The owner feared AI answering would reduce reservations — actual reservations did not decline, and bookings now come in outside opening hours too. The source gives no data on labor-hours or cost savings. (Provenance: vendor-reported — IVRy customer case, owner's own account.)

**Firm profile**: Tiny restaurants and izakayas where a few people run everything, phones can't be answered at peak, and reservations arrive mainly by phone.
