# Case 20.6 (United States, FAILURE CASE): Oil & gas — Pioneer's ML well-completion PoC yielded limited gains: too little data, and the model treated well names as key features

**Industry / scenario**: (Large enterprise: Pioneer Natural Resources, 2,213 employees as of 2023-12-31) Permian Basin shale-oil well-completion design optimization using machine learning to predict production.

**Pain point**: Pioneer wanted to know whether any vendor's machine learning could improve its Permian well-completion strategy, so it ran a proof-of-concept competition among five analytics vendors.

**Result**: The Journal of Petroleum Technology report describes mixed results: with only ~400 wells' data, model accuracy was much lower than hoped, and the model ranked well *names* among the key predictive features — a red flag that it was memorizing identifiers rather than learning physics. The exercise exposed how data volume and quality constrain ML in completion design. The handbook files this under "never validated on your own data and conditions."

*Sources: Journal of Petroleum Technology (SPE), "Pioneer's Analytics Project Reveals the Good and Bad of Machine Learning" by Trent Jacobs (2018-07-25); scale: Pioneer company materials (independent industry publication)*
