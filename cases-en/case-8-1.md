# Case 8.1 (United States): Education — Georgia State University cut "summer melt" with the Pounce SMS AI assistant

**Industry / scenario**: (Public institution: a U.S. public university; enrollment not given in cited sources) Georgia State University, addressing "summer melt" — admitted students who never show up for fall classes.

**Pain point**: Per media coverage, the university's summer melt had risen from 12% toward 19% in recent years; answering the flood of student messages manually would have required ~10 additional full-time staff.

**AI approach**: With startup AdmitHub, GSU launched "Pounce," an SMS chatbot named after the mascot, proactively reminding students about transcripts and deadlines and answering questions about tuition, housing and enrollment steps.

**Outcome**: Per the researchers' published abstract, treatment students were 3.3 percentage points more likely to enroll on time, with summer melt reduced 21% (academic study). Media coverage gives melt 21.4% lower than control and enrollment 3.9% higher. Pounce exchanged nearly 200,000 messages with treatment students; fewer than 1% of the 50,000 student-sent messages needed staff attention (media; vendor Mainstay says "under 2%"). GSU continued and expanded the deployment.

**Firm profile**: Universities, training providers and service firms with concentrated inquiry volume and procedural questions that need "deadline reminders + FAQ answers" at scale.

*Sources: Campus Technology "Using AI Chatbots to Freeze 'Summer Melt' in Higher Ed" (2017-03-07) (media); Brookings "Freezing 'summer melt' in its tracks" (research summary) (independent research); vendor Mainstay materials (vendor-reported)*
