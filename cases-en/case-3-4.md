# Case 3.4 (Hong Kong, China): Finance (Brokerage) — A licensed brokerage replaces night-shift human agents with LLM-powered customer service, cutting the customer complaint rate by 80%

**Industry / scenario**: (Size undisclosed: a licensed corporation of the Hong Kong Securities and Futures Commission (SFC); total headcount not public; SFC register data compiled by a third party shows 9 current licensed individuals) Securities brokerage; 24/7 online customer service for overseas clients and an AI investor relations officer.

**Pain point**: Waton Securities International Limited (华通证券国际有限公司) is a licensed brokerage with 33 years of experience in the securities industry. Overseas clients' inquiries often involve large amounts of account information and trading data; without timely replies, clients feel insecure and their trust in the company drops sharply. Before deployment, overseas customer service required 2 daytime agents and 5 night-time agents.

**AI solution**: Built an AI customer service system by calling Anthropic's Claude 3 models through Amazon Web Services' Amazon Bedrock: Claude Sonnet and Haiku identify customer intent, and replies are generated automatically using an in-house knowledge base and an open-source embedding model; human agents only need to review and enhance the content generated by the model. An AI investor relations officer was launched at the same time. The whole process — from initial contact, POC testing and architecture deployment to go-live — took less than 3 months.

**Results**: Customer service response time fell from several minutes to within 3 seconds, with inquiries handled 24/7; the customer complaint rate fell by 80%; 3 human agents working alongside the AI customer service can now handle the overseas customer service work previously done, cutting overall operating costs by 36.6% (Basis: vendor-reported — article bylined by Amazon Web Services and published via TMTPost).

**Who this fits**: Small and mid-sized brokerages, futures companies and cross-border financial service providers whose clients span multiple time zones, who need overnight coverage, and whose inquiries are mainly about accounts and trades.
