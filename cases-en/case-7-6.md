# Case 7.6 (United States, FAILURE CASE): Healthcare — external validation of Epic's sepsis prediction model: it missed 67% of patients and alerted on 18% of hospitalizations

**Industry / scenario**: (Size undisclosed: proprietary model from EMR vendor Epic, headcount not public; deployed in hundreds of U.S. hospitals) The Epic Sepsis Model built into U.S. hospital EMR systems.

**Pain point**: Sepsis requires early recognition and antibiotics; hospitals hoped an EMR-embedded model would flag it in time.

**Result**: The external validation (JAMA Internal Medicine 2021;181(8), Wong et al.) found the model missed 67% of sepsis patients while alerting on 18% of all hospitalized patients — far below the vendor's reported performance. The handbook files this under "never validated on your own data and conditions." Epic issued a statement disputing aspects of the study (via Healthcare IT News coverage).

*Sources: Wong A, et al., "External Validation of a Widely Implemented Proprietary Sepsis Prediction Model in Hospitalized Patients," JAMA Internal Medicine 2021;181(8) (PMC full text) (independent research); Healthcare IT News "Research suggests Epic Sepsis Model is lacking in predictive power" (Kat Jercich, 2021-06-22) (media, includes Epic statement)*
