# Case 18.2 (Japan): Pharmaceutical R&D — Chugai Pharmaceutical builds an in-house generative AI platform used by 1,000–1,500 people a day

**Industry / scenario**: (Large enterprise: 7,778 employees on a consolidated basis, as of December 31, 2024) Chugai Pharmaceutical. The scenario is a pharmaceutical company using generative AI in R&D, clinical development and regulatory submission documents.

**Pain point**: Takao Suzuki (鈴木孝雄), Counselor (参与) and head of the DX division at Chugai Pharmaceutical, pointed out that the pharmaceutical industry as a whole faces two problems: the cost of creating a new drug keeps rising, and R&D productivity keeps falling. According to the report, clinical trials plus regulatory review for a new drug take about 96.8 months, while the clinical-trial success rate is only about 11.8% (industry data cited by the speaker).

**AI solution**: With AWS Bedrock at its core, the company built "Chugai Cloud Infrastracture," a multi-cloud environment that can use multiple cloud platforms, in which multiple generative AI models can be selected by use case to develop services and applications. It developed its own in-house assistant, "Chugai AI Assistant," combined with retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). Envisioned uses include literature search in R&D, exploration of target molecules, design of long-term clinical plans, drafting support for trial-related documents and various applications, and draft responses to inquiries from the PMDA.

**Results**: Chugai AI Assistant releases new features every week and is currently used by 1,000–1,500 people a day. The company solicited ideas for generative AI applications internally and collected about 900 within a few months of starting; of these, about 29 have a PoC under way or completed, and 14 are in full development or have finished development. (Basis: company self-reported, via a media report, from a case-study presentation on November 21, 2024. No benefit figures such as work hours saved were disclosed; in the report, the clinical and regulatory uses are envisioned, not results already in production.)

**Who this fits**: Pharmaceutical companies with several thousand employees and heavy R&D and clinical documentation workloads that want to use multiple models side by side on a unified platform, starting by soliciting use cases internally and screening them for PoCs.
