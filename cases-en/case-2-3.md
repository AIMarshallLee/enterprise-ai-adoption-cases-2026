# Case 2.3 (China, German-owned company): Manufacturing — A German-owned PCB maker's Jiangyou plant uses AI for dynamic production scheduling, cutting delays by 43%

**Industry / scenario**: (Large enterprise: STARTEAM GLOBAL has 1,000+ employees worldwide and 800+ at its plant in Jiangyou, Sichuan) PCB (printed circuit board) manufacturing / AI production scheduling for high-mix, low-volume production.

**Pain point**: The sources do not describe a specific pain point; they only state that PAILOT's solution targets high-mix, low-volume manufacturing environments.

**AI solution**: STARTEAM GLOBAL's PCB plant in Jiangyou, Sichuan adopted PAILOT's AI production planning platform, which integrates with the plant's existing MES/ERP via API and performs real-time dynamic scheduling in response to order changes, equipment availability and production disruptions; STARTEAM says PAILOT's technology has been integrated into its drilling and routing equipment and its MES.

**Results**: Delays down 43%, capacity utilization up 10%, changeover (setup) time down 15%, energy consumption down 24%. The talk title says "Increased Utilization by up to 20%", which is inconsistent with the 10% in the body of the abstract; the source does not explain the discrepancy. (Basis: vendor-reported — abstract of the joint PAILOT and STARTEAM talk at productronica 2025)

**Who this fits**: High-mix, low-volume PCB / electronics manufacturing plants that already run MES/ERP and want to raise utilization and reduce delays without adding equipment.

*Sources: productronica 2025 talk abstract, "AI That Delivers: How STARTEAM Increased Utilization by up to 20% without Investing in New Equipment" (2025-11-18) (vendor-reported); STARTEAM GLOBAL electronica 2026 company brochure (2026) (company self-reported); STARTEAM GLOBAL in I-Connect007, "Growth Unfolds: The First of STARTEAM's Trio of Factories Set to Expand" (2024-12-05) (company self-reported)*
