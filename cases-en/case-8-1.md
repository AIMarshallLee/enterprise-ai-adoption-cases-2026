# Case 8.1 (United States): Education — Georgia State University uses the text-message AI assistant Pounce to reduce "summer melt"

**Industry / scenario**: (Public institution: a US public university; student enrollment not disclosed in the cited sources) Georgia State University (GSU), tackling "summer melt" — newly admitted students who drop off between admission and the start of the fall semester.

**Pain point**: According to media reports, the university's summer melt rate had risen in recent years from 12% to nearly 19%; answering students' large volume of messages by hand would have required the university to add about 10 full-time staff.

**AI solution**: Working with the startup AdmitHub, GSU launched Pounce, a text-message chatbot named after the campus mascot. It proactively reminded students to submit materials such as transcripts and to watch deadlines, and answered common questions about tuition deadlines, FAFSA financial-aid applications, housing and so on; a small number of questions were handed over to the university's advisors. It launched in April 2016 as a randomized controlled trial: half of the more than 7,000 newly admitted students received text messages from Pounce.

**Results**: According to the abstract of the paper published by the researchers, students in the treatment group were 3.3 percentage points more likely to enroll on time, and summer melt fell by 21% (academic research figures). The media-reported figures are: the treatment group's summer melt rate was 21.4% lower than the control group's, and its enrollment rate was 3.9% higher. Pounce exchanged nearly 200,000 messages with treatment-group students; of the 50,000 messages students sent, fewer than 1% needed handling by university staff (media report; the vendor Mainstay puts it at less than 2%). The university subsequently said it would keep using Pounce and expand its uses.

**Who this fits**: Universities, training providers and service businesses with concentrated inquiry volumes and many transactional questions, which need reminders on where an application or process stands plus answers to common questions.
