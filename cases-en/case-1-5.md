# Case 1.5 (Australia, FAILURE CASE): Workforce training — Treasury's Copilot pilot: inflated expectations, insufficient training, usage below projections

**Industry / scenario**: (Government agency) The Australian Treasury's pilot of a generative-AI office assistant (Microsoft 365 Copilot).

**Pain point**: Treasury wanted a 14-week pilot, evaluated by an independent evaluator, to establish how much value a generative-AI office assistant actually delivers in day-to-day departmental work.

**Result**: The pilot found usage fell well short of projections — reported usage dropped from 75% of staff in early weeks to 38%, and weekly active usage from 15% to 2% (figures via ACS Information Age coverage of the evaluation). The handbook classifies this among expectation-overhang / insufficient-training failures.

*Sources: Australian Treasury, "Evaluation of a trial of generative AI (Copilot) in The Treasury" (Feb 2025); Australian Centre for Evaluation, summary and full report (Feb 2025) (government); Information Age (ACS), "Treasury releases Microsoft Copilot trial results" (2025-02-13) (media, used only for the 75%→38% and 15%→2% figures)*

> Provenance note: self-reported and vendor-reported figures are not verified by third parties — treat them as upper bounds. From *Enterprise AI Adoption Case Handbook (October 2026 edition)*, compiled by Kunlun Growth with number-by-number verification against primary sources.
