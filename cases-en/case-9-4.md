# Case 9.4 (Japan): Government — a 42,000-resident city measured generative-AI impact across staff, projecting ~40,000 hours saved per year

**Industry / scenario**: (Government: Shima City, Mie Prefecture — population 42,240 per the city website as of 2026-09-30; projection based on 445 staff with Google Workspace accounts) Scenario: everyday drafting, document summarization, information retrieval and research by city staff.

**Pain point**: The press release names no standalone pain point. The city states the goal of moving time saved from clerical work into citizen-facing services — citizen inquiries and higher-quality policy planning.

**AI approach**: Staff use Google Workspace with generative AI (Gemini). The Policy Promotion Division measured adoption effects across all staff. The release does not state the deployment start date.

**Outcome**: Staff use generative AI on 2.4 days per week on average — established as a working tool; each task shortens by 7–14 minutes on average. Per person per week: ~20.4 minutes saved on drafting, ~11.9 on summarization, ~21.2 on retrieval and research. FY Reiwa 7 (Apr 2025–Mar 2026) projection: 40,000 hours saved per year, ~50 hours per person — equivalent to 20 staff members' labor. (Provenance: government self-reported projection, based on actual usage by 445 account holders; the release does not explain the conversion between 40,000 hours, 50 hours/person and 445 staff.)

**Firm profile**: Local governments and public agencies rolling out generative AI office-wide with measurement — the model here is instrumenting usage before claiming savings.

*Source: Shima City press release (2026, effect measurement based on 445 Google Workspace staff accounts) (government self-reported projection)*
