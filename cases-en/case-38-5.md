# Case 38.5 (Japan): Legal services — a 4-lawyer Kobe firm uses AI contract review; the lawyer feels 5-hour reviews now take 10 minutes

**Industry / scenario**: (Small enterprise: 4 attorneys, support staff undisclosed) Kobe Marine Sogo Law Office (弁護士法人神戸マリン綜合法律事務所), Hyogo Prefecture. Practice centers on corporate law such as contract review; retainer clients include care facilities, dental clinics and temples. Scenario: contract review and drafting.

**Pain point**: Contract review used to mean switching between Word and several other windows.

**AI approach**: The firm had used LegalForce and, after seeing a LegalOn demo, switched to LegalOn as closer to how lawyers actually work.

**Outcome**: The lawyer feels that reviews that took about 5 hours now take about 10 minutes — but this is the lawyer's own perception, not a measured value. (Provenance: vendor-reported.)

**Firm profile**: Solo and small legal practices doing corporate contract work, where the volume of review is the bottleneck and perception-level gains still justify tooling.
