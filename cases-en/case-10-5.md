# Case 10.5 (Japan): Energy — a 128-person LPG supplier uses AI for level forecasting and auto-routing, so novices can start delivering sooner

**Industry / scenario**: (Mid-size: Tosa Gas's recruitment listing states 128 employees (Oct 2025) and ¥2.1B revenue (June 2025); SoftBank's case page labels it 101–500 employees) Tosa Gas, founded 1963, supplies LPG in Kochi Prefecture from three filling stations. Scenario: LPG delivery level forecasting and route planning.

**Pain point**: Delivery planning relied on individual experience, and drivers are aging — whether delivery could continue stably after retirements was uncertain. Drivers manually planned routes from weekly customer lists, balancing distance, sequence and load, 30–60 minutes per day. Paper delivery slips with personal information meant heavy printing costs and management burden.

**AI approach**: From April 2024, SoftBank's "Routify": AI forecasts each household's gas level from existing meter and delivery data, determines the right delivery timing, and auto-computes routes. The group is pushing smart gas meters to 55%+ of customers.

**Outcome**: All 7 drivers use the system daily; paper and printing costs fell and lost-slip risk dropped (no specific figures). (Provenance: vendor-reported — SoftBank official customer case, interview ~6 months in.)

**Firm profile**: Regional LPG and fuel distributors with meter data whose delivery depends on veteran staff — start with "level forecasting + auto-routing" to reduce dependence on personal experience.

*Sources: SoftBank "Tosa Gas" customer case (2025-07) (vendor-reported); Mynavi 2027 corporate page (updated 2026-07-03) (self-filled recruitment listing, scale basis)*
